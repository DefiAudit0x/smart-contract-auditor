Methodology and limits

Use analysis as evidence, not a security guarantee.

AuditAI combines available source analysis and model-assisted review to help security teams prioritize questions. It cannot prove that a contract is safe, exploitable, or ready for deployment.

Required review step: validate every reported issue against the relevant source, compiler version, deployment configuration, privileges, dependencies, and call graph.

What the workspace can help with

It supports intake from code, repositories, archives, and diffs, then presents available checks and generated reports in a common review flow.

  • Prioritizing potential risk patterns.
  • Comparing revision-level changes.
  • Documenting questions and report evidence.

What a result cannot establish

A result does not replace an independent audit or prove a complete absence of vulnerabilities.

  • Coverage may be limited by source, dependency, compiler, or proxy context.
  • Static patterns do not always establish exploitability.
  • Generated fixes and PoCs require independent review.

How to interpret benchmark figures

Benchmark results describe the stated corpus and its detector configuration. They are not universal precision, recall, or production-risk guarantees.

Operational responsibilities

Operators remain responsible for access control, secrets, TLS, rate limiting, upload policy, logging, model-provider configuration, and human review before remediation or release.
