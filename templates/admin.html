Learned Patterns — PoC Confirmation

PoC gate, not a text note: a candidate becomes an active detector only when every regex in it fires on the vulnerable sample below and none fires on the fixed sample. Text evidence alone never confirms a learned pattern.

Not the KB confirm route: the separate API route /api/admin/patterns/confirm confirms knowledge-base patterns by integer id on text evidence only. Those are RAG context only — they are never executed as detectors and are never PoC confirmation. Do not present a KB confirmation as proof of a detector.

Removal: deleting a confirmed pattern from learned_patterns.json takes effect at the next pre-scan — no process restart is needed. A pattern that is still listed here is a candidate and is inactive.

Contention fails closed: PoC checks share a pool of 4 isolated regex workers. If the pool is busy, a confirmation may fail without confirming anything; retry it when workers free up rather than weakening the gate.
